# DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on sm_120: step 42's m-tile loops and an FC2 grid cap set for serving — 2026-11-13

What was measured on the reference host when two runtime keys of step 42 were set for serving, with the image
unchanged and the server not restarted: three paired series inside the serving server — the m-tile loops, FC2
capped at 256 CTAs, the m-tile loops under that cap —, the rule for which keys stay, the bits in the state that
stayed, six series on that state with no other request in flight against the state before it, and where in a
163K prefill the gain sits. Then what is not known and not claimed, and what the same two levers read on the second
host and on a single card: a reference for whoever measures such a lever somewhere else. The switches and their
served state are in [`docs/dsv41-sm120.md`](../dsv41-sm120.md#sub-switches); what the two levers do is in
[the internals](../dsv41-sm120-internals.md#step-42--the-prefill-moe-kernels).

**What a unit ran.** The launch counters of the ranks say what the states of a unit were made of. In every unit of
the three series the attention chunks ran on `v43c`: 1 792 – 1 808 launches per unit, none on the shipped cubin. A
unit makes 896 calls of the prefill MoE, every one with FC1 on the warpgroup kernel and the top-k sum as work items
of FC2 — the calls of 512 rows or more, the eager layers of the pipelined step. The 128-row replay layers of a step
make no call: they run from CUDA graphs captured when the server started, and a captured launch keeps the kernel
and the grid it was captured with. A runtime key does not reach it. In every state of these series, and in the
adopted state, the replay layers therefore run step 42's kernels as the environment sets them — without the m-tile
loops, FC2 on its full grid. What was measured is the two levers on the eager calls.

## The rule and the state that stayed

The rule was fixed before the series. A series passes when it ended with every key back in the state before, both
of its states ran on the cubin they must, its 163K reading by order is "faster" and its 19K reading is not
"slower". Both levers stay when the series of the cap and the series of the loops under the cap pass. If the cap's
passes and the other does not, one lever stays: the cap, or the loops when their own series passes with a gain at
163K whose interval lies wholly beyond the cap's. If the cap's does not pass, the loops stay when their own series
passes, and nothing otherwise. All three series passed, each "faster" at both prompt lengths.

The keys `B12X_MOE_PREFILL_FAST_MT_LOOPS` = 1 and `B12X_MOE_PREFILL_FAST_GRID2` = 256 were written beside
`SPARSE_MLA_PREFILL_SASS_CUBIN_SEL` = 1, the change confirmed on the four ranks, long_agree and agree taken in that
state ([Bits](#bits)), and the control file removed. A removed file keeps the state it last set: the four ranks
report those three keys and every other key at its environment's value, and no control file exists. The state
belongs to the running server. A start comes up in the environment's state — the shipped cubin with `v43c` loaded
beside it, no m-tile loops, FC2 on its full grid — until the keys are written again.

## What is not known, and what is not claimed

- **The first step.** Whether the first step of a 163K prefill costs more in the adopted state is not resolved: the
  clean runs read +3 ms by the pooled medians (95 %: +0 … +13 ms) and +5 ms by the means, and the series of one
  state differ by more than that. A cost between 0 and about 13 ms; the first step is not claimed to be unchanged.
- **19K.** The paired series read −1.2 … −1.3 % with intervals of 0.6 – 1.1; the clean series' medians overlap
  between the two states. No 19K number is claimed for the adopted state against the state before.
- **The two levers as one pair.** The cap alone and the loops under the cap are two series, −1.943 % and −1.131 %.
  The two levers together against the state before were not measured as one pair; the one number for them is the
  clean series' −2.56 %, taken between two states of one server at different hours.
- **One set of series, one server life.** Three series of 6 – 8 pairs beside other clients' requests, six clean
  series afterwards, on one day in one lifetime of one server.
- **The card model and the limit.** Every number above is the reference host's: RTX PRO 6000 Workstation Edition
  at a 400 W limit, the cards in the power cap. No number is claimed for another card model or another limit; the
  last section shows how little of these two levers a second host at the same limit sees.
- **A server started with the levers.** The levers were measured as runtime keys written into a running server,
  which leaves the launches captured at its start as they were. A server started with
  `VLLM_MOET_B12X_MOE_PREFILL_FAST_MT_LOOPS=1` and `VLLM_MOET_B12X_MOE_PREFILL_FAST_GRID2=256` in its environment
  captures them with the levers. That state has no series and no bit check here.
- **Decode.** No decode series was taken in the adopted state.
- **The lower SM clock** of the state with the lever, and the energy per run, whose intervals are as wide as the
  effects, are measured and not explained.
