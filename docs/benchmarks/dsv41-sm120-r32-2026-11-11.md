# DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on sm_120: the served image from `-r28` to `-r32` — 2026-11-11

What was measured on the reference host when the served image changed from `-r28` to `-r32`: the same series on
the old server and on the new one within one hour, the bits, a kernel check on the four cards, what the new server
logged, the prefill MoE kernels of `-r28` against the set before them inside the serving server, the host memory,
and a series on the new server alone afterwards. What the image adds is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md); the
switches and their served state are in [`docs/dsv41-sm120.md`](../dsv41-sm120.md#switches).

| name here | what |

## The same series on the old and on the new server

One hour, neither series idle (up to three other requests in flight in both), so the two columns carry the same
disturbance and neither is an idle number.

| what | runs | the old server (`-r28`) | the new server (`-r32`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| fresh prefill, 163K tokens, median (range) | 8 | 5 535.5 ms (5 435 – 5 973) | 5 517 ms (5 434 – 5 606) |
| fresh prefill, 19K tokens, median (range) | 8 | 667.5 ms (663 – 675) | 671.5 ms (658 – 686) |
| energy per 163K run, four cards | 8 | 9 318 J | 9 164 J |
| Fraser bench (2,048 tokens out, temperature 0), median | 10 | 129.30 steps/s | 127.30 steps/s |
| fresh prefill 163K under 100 nice'd CPU hogs on the server's cores, median | 3 | — | 5 943 ms (+7.7 % against its 5 517 ms) |

- **163K**: the new server is −0.3 % against the old one in the same hour and +1.4 % against the old image's idle
  median of 5 443 ms ([the 2026-11-10 record](dsv41-sm120-steps-2026-11-10.md#as-served)); the acceptance limit of the
  change was +2 % against that idle median. The two images run the same prefill kernels bit for bit, and two
  lifetimes of one server differ by up to 1 %.
- **Decode**: the new server's Fraser series ran two minutes after it became ready, right behind its prefill
  series. Every change of image measured in this position so far has shown −1.2 … −1.5 %; the series on the new
  server alone ([below](#the-new-server-alone)) is the number to use.
- **Energy**: the 19K runs are too short for the reading — the same server reads 1 836 J per 19K run in this series
  and 816 J in the series below — so only the 163K energy is given.

## The prefill MoE kernels in the serving server

The runtime keys of step 42b switched the new server between two kernel sets without a restart: **A** = the served
state (`-r28`'s kernels: FC1 on the warpgroup kernel, the top-k sum as work items of the FC2 kernel), **B** = the
kernels of `-r26b` (keys `B12X_MOE_PREFILL_FAST_WS1` 0 and `B12X_MOE_PREFILL_FAST_SUM_ITEMS` 0). One unit is a 19K
and a 163K fresh prefill; 24 valid pairs of units in alternating order (AB, BA, …), a pair with another request in
flight repeated (1 rejected); the state of every unit confirmed on the four ranks.

| prompt | A, median (range) | B, median (range) | A − B, mean of the pairs ± 95 % interval | A faster in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 163K | 5 808 ms (5 689 – 5 872) | 5 950 ms (5 845 – 6 006) | −2.24 % ± 0.20 % | 24 of 24 pairs |
| 19K | 697 ms (681 – 704) | 714 ms (703 – 725) | −2.45 % ± 0.37 % | 24 of 24 pairs |

The comparison is inside the pairs; the absolute times belong to this run. The same keys on the second host, the
image as built: −3.32 % ± 0.11 % at 163K (12 pairs). The key between the two attention cubins was not run.

The old image's idle series under the same governor — 5 443 ms, 666.5 ms and 128.5 steps/s

compared here.

## The Fraser bench, 40 reps

About 110 minutes after the new server became ready: 40 runs in eight groups of five, back to back, a group counted
only when no other request was in flight during it (all eight were), the cards at 41–43 °C when the series starts.

| what | value |
|---|---|
| steps/s | median 128.35, mean 128.50 (127.20 – 130.20) |
| tok/s out | median 671.5, best 724.5 (615.6 – 724.5) |
| acceptance | median 0.608 (0.54 – 0.67); mean accepted length 5.25 |
| time to the first token | median 126.6 ms |
| steps/s by group (the hottest card at the group's start) | 129.50 (43 °C), 129.20 (50), 128.60 (55), 128.40 (59), 128.10 (62), 127.90 (65), 127.50 (68), 127.80 (68) |
| tok/s median by group | 696.0, 683.9, 699.4, 663.9, 666.1, 643.6, 673.2, 675.9 |

The first two groups (cards at 43–50 °C) read 129.50 steps/s and 690.0 tok/s; the old server's ten runs at the start
of the change, on cool cards, read 129.30 and 691.6. Steps/s falls by 1.5 % as the cards warm from 43 to 68 °C, and
the tok/s median of a series follows the acceptance of its runs (every run's prompt begins with a fresh nonce line).
