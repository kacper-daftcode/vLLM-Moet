# Inkling-2-Small on sm_120: the measurement record of 2026-11-13

What stands behind the numbers of [docs/inkling2-sm120.md](../inkling2-sm120.md): each kernel against the
operation's definition on one RTX PRO 6000, a six-layer cut of the checkpoint on one card and on four, the full
checkpoint on four cards (TP4, experts expert-parallel). **Every served measurement of the full model is of the
checkpoint with the experts of layers 40–47 repacked from MXFP8 to e2m3, six bits per value** - 62–65 % of those
weights exact, the rest within −38 to −39 dB (section 3), 5.7 GiB less per card; the gate against the released
weights is section 7 (six layers on one card, both ways: the same greedy tokens) and the full model's GSM8K
(193 / 200 in three runs). At the released precision the model does not fit four 96 GB cards beside a paged cache.
Software: the image `vllm/vllm-openai:inkling-2-small`
(vLLM `0.1.dev25249+gb974b306f`, torch 2.14.0, FlashInfer 0.7.3, Triton 3.8.0, CUDA 13.0) with the steps of
`Dockerfile.sm120-inkling2`; RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (capability 12.0, 188 SMs, 95.0 GiB usable, 400 W), driver
610.43.02. Every test is in `tools/inkling2_sm120/` and prints these lines.

## 1. The operand layouts of the block-scaled MMAs

`bmm/test_qmma_layout_sm120.py`: one `mma.sync` with every lane's registers given by the host, for
`kind::mxf4nvf4` `scale_vec::4X` m16n8k64 (e2m1 × e2m1, ue4m3 scales), `kind::mxf8f6f4` `scale_vec::1X` m16n8k32
(e4m3 × e4m3, ue8m0) and the same with an e2m3 B operand, against exact arithmetic of the decoded operands:
**2560 of 2560 outputs equal.** The layouts: A fragment a0 / a2 = row q, a1 / a3 = row q + 8 (q = lane / 4, bytes
4c.. and 16 + 4c.. of the row's 32-byte step, c = lane % 4); B fragment b0 / b1 = column q; the A scale word from
row q in even lanes and row q + 8 in odd lanes; the B scale word from column q; the C fragment (row q, columns
2c, 2c + 1) and (row q + 8, the same columns).

## 4. The relative attention (step 2)

`attn/test_attn_sm120.py`: **43 of 43 cases** against the float64 definition with everything the kernel must not
read set to NaN - decode of full layers (1 to 94 splits) and window layers (1 to 9), padding requests, the draft
mode with and without a window, multi-request prefill with and without context, page sizes 16 / 32 / 128, GQA groups
6 and 4. Relative RMS error 0.9e-3 to 2.2e-3 (the softmax weights are rounded to bf16 before P @ V, as in vLLM's
kernels; the decode output itself is bf16), LSE within 1.2e-6; no NaN in an output; two runs bit-identical. The
kernel: 65 536 B of shared memory, 255 registers without a stack frame, one CTA per SM.

## 10. The DSpark draft

The checkpoint's draft (`mtp.safetensors`: five dense layers over the target's features of eight depths, a block of
an anchor and seven masks, a recurrent Markov head), `--speculative-config '{"method":"dspark",
"num_speculative_tokens":7,"draft_sample_method":"probabilistic"}'`.

### What it needed beside steps 1–7

Each operation of the draft's path that is not Inkling2's own Triton kernel, called on a card in a process of its
own (a launch without a kernel image leaves a sticky CUDA error):

| operation | on capability 12 |
|---|---|
| `reshape_and_cache_flash` of vLLM's compiled library (the draft's K / V write) | "no kernel image is available for execution on the device" |
| vLLM's Triton kernel of the same operation | the same bytes at the same slots |
| FlashInfer's `rmsnorm` and `fused_add_rmsnorm` (the draft's norms) | within bf16 rounding of the definition |
| `torch.addmm` of bf16 operands with an fp32 result (the Markov head) | 5.7e-5 of the definition |

Step 8 routes the first to the second (`test_cache_flash_sm120.py`: 24 cases - both cache layouts, 1 to 1024 tokens,
a batch longer than the slot mapping, every other byte of a random cache untouched).
