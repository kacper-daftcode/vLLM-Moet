# Inkling-2-Small on sm_120: the measurement record of 2026-11-13

**Every served measurement of the full model in sections 9 and 10 is of the checkpoint with the experts of layers
40–47 repacked from MXFP8 to e2m3, six bits per value - a lossy change of the released weights**: 76–79 % of those
weights keep their value, the largest error is 1 / 64 of a block's largest magnitude, the error energy −38 to −39 dB
of the weight energy (section 3); 5.7 GiB less per card. Against the released weights there are two comparisons:
six layers on one card served both ways return the same greedy tokens (section 7), and the full model served both
ways in one configuration - an 8K context, 64-token chunks and no draft, in which the released precision has 0.69 GiB
of paged cache and the packed form 6.38 - answers 195 of 200 GSM8K questions as released and 194 packed, the same
answer to 197, with 19 of 24 greedy answers the same text (section 11). Not measured: the released precision beyond
8K tokens or with the draft.

What stands behind the numbers of [docs/inkling2-sm120.md](../inkling2-sm120.md): each kernel against the
operation's definition on one RTX PRO 6000, a six-layer cut of the checkpoint on one card and on four, the full
checkpoint on four cards (TP4, experts expert-parallel).
Software: the image `vllm/vllm-openai:inkling-2-small`
(vLLM `0.1.dev25249+gb974b306f`, torch 2.14.0, FlashInfer 0.7.3, Triton 3.8.0, CUDA 13.0) with the steps of
`Dockerfile.sm120-inkling2`; RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (capability 12.0, 188 SMs, 95.0 GiB usable, 400 W), driver
610.43.02. Every test is in `tools/inkling2_sm120/` and prints these lines.

## 1. The operand layouts of the block-scaled MMAs

`bmm/test_qmma_layout_sm120.py`: one `mma.sync` with every lane's registers given by the host, for
`kind::mxf4nvf4` `scale_vec::4X` m16n8k64 (e2m1 × e2m1, ue4m3 scales), `kind::mxf8f6f4` `scale_vec::1X` m16n8k32
(e4m3 × e4m3, ue8m0) and the same with an e2m3 B operand, against exact arithmetic of the decoded operands:
**2560 of 2560 outputs equal.** The layouts: A fragment a0 / a2 = row q, a1 / a3 = row q + 8 (q = lane / 4, bytes
4c.. and 16 + 4c.. of the row's 32-byte step, c = lane % 4); B fragment b0 / b1 = column q; the A scale word from
row q in even lanes and row q + 8 in odd lanes; the B scale word from column q; the C fragment (row q, columns
2c, 2c + 1) and (row q + 8, the same columns).

## 3. MXFP8 experts as e2m3 (`pack_e2m3`)

`bmm/test_pack_e2m3.py --model-dir <checkpoint> --census 12` on the CPU: 12 experts of each of the layers 40–47
(indices spread over the layer's 384), both projections - one in sixteen of the repacked experts. The energies are
in the weights' own units, every block times its scale.

| | gate / up (w13), 96 experts | down (w2), 96 experts |
|---|---|---|
| weights that keep their value exactly | 76.22–76.41 % | 77.47–78.58 % |
| of them in the block's top three binades (exact by construction) | 65.72–65.98 % | 67.54–69.09 % |
| error energy / weight energy | −38.4 to −38.3 dB | −39.4 to −38.7 dB |
| largest error / the block's largest magnitude | 1 / 64 | 1 / 64 |

A weight below the block's top three binades is rounded to a grid of 1 / 32 of the block's largest binade's lower
end and can become zero: the bound is on the error against the block's largest weight, not against the weight.

The packing cases of `test_bmm_sm120.py` on a card, experts 0–3 of layer 40: the kernel's output with e2m3 weights
against its output with the e4m3 weights on the same random activations differs by 1.23e-2 (−38.2 dB, gate / up)
and 1.11e-2 (−39.1 dB, down) relative. That run printed 62.10 % and 64.70 % of the weights as exact with the same
−38.4 / −39.3 dB and 1 / 64: it compared values scaled by a floating power of two computed on the card. The
packing's bytes are the same on the CPU, where the same four experts give 76.33 % and 78.49 %; with its powers of
two built from bits the test prints those two figures on the card as well.

A six-layer cut does not produce text (it repeats prompt tokens); it is a vehicle for the kernels' paths and for
paired comparisons, not a quality reading.

How the gaps of the base image were found. Reading vLLM's code named the expert GEMM, both attention kernels, the
small-batch linear, the draft's argmax and the fused all-reduce, and their steps were written before anything was
started. The unpatched image itself was never started on a capability-12 card; what its expert GEMM does there was
seen in this image with every runtime switch on vLLM's own path (`VLLM_MOET_INKLING2_BMM=0`, `ATTN=0`, `LINEAR=0`, the
fused embedding, vLLM's argmax, the compiled K / V write, every draft graph): a start of this cut ends after 333 s
with exit code 1 in the construction of the first decoder layer's experts - `Inkling2Experts.__init__` →
`load_kernels` → `expert_plan` → FlashInfer's `get_nvcc_flags_list`: `RuntimeError: No supported CUDA architectures
found for major versions [10].` No weight is loaded before it; most of the 333 s is FlashInfer compiling its
`fused_moe_trtllm_sm100` module (a first such start was stopped at 240 s, still compiling). The attention kernels, the
linear and the argmax are not reached by that start: read, not seen. The rest were failures on a card with those steps
in place: the vision forward (its compile) and the router's shared memory (the profile run) on this cut on one card,
the fused embedding with the same cut on four cards (the first warm-up step), the draft's K / V write (section 10) by
calling each operation of the draft's path that is not the model's own Triton kernel alone in a process, before the
draft was started.

## 10. The DSpark draft

The checkpoint's draft (`mtp.safetensors`: five dense layers over the target's features of eight depths, a block of
an anchor and seven masks, a recurrent Markov head), `--speculative-config '{"method":"dspark",
"num_speculative_tokens":7,"draft_sample_method":"probabilistic"}'`.

### What it needed beside steps 1–7

Each operation of the draft's path that is not Inkling2's own Triton kernel, called on a card in a process of its
own (a launch without a kernel image leaves a sticky CUDA error):

| operation | on capability 12 |
|---|---|
| `reshape_and_cache_flash` of vLLM's compiled library (the draft's K / V write) | "no kernel image is available for execution on the device" |
| vLLM's Triton kernel of the same operation | the same bytes at the same slots |
| FlashInfer's `rmsnorm` and `fused_add_rmsnorm` (the draft's norms) | within bf16 rounding of the definition |
| `torch.addmm` of bf16 operands with an fp32 result (the Markov head) | 5.7e-5 of the definition |

Step 8 routes the first to the second (`test_cache_flash_sm120.py`: 24 cases - both cache layouts, 1 to 1024 tokens,
a batch longer than the slot mapping, every other byte of a random cache untouched).

### On one card

The six-layer cut with the draft attached (`make_mini_checkpoint.py --layers 0,1,2,42,43,41 --draft`: the draft keeps
the two of its eight features the cut has), TP1, 8 sequences. The draft of a cut predicts nothing (0.8 % of 28 000
drafted tokens accepted), so this reads that every kernel of the path runs - the draft's load (54.4 GiB with it), its
K / V write, its block attention (step 2's draft mode, a window of 1032 keys), the Markov head, the target's
verification of 8 rows per request, rejection - not a rate.

| | |
|---|---|
